In a few minutes Leapla is set up the way you want it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.leapla.co](https://app.leapla.co/register) and sign up with your email address. You get an email to confirm your address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go through the start">
    Leapla asks you a few short questions: your name, your language and country, what you use Leapla for (private, work or both) and what you want to take care of. You can skip every question except your name.

    At the end Leapla sets up what you chose. Your menu then shows the pages that fit your choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your way">
    - **The black strip on the left** shows your worlds: Personal at the top, then your businesses, then your shared workspaces. At the bottom are the Inbox, Help and your account.
    - **The menu next to it** shows the pages of the world you are in.
    - **At the top** you search everything, and open **Apla**, the assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust your menu">
    - **Add a page:** click the **+** below the menu.
    - **Remove a page:** right-click it (on a phone: press and hold). Your data is kept.
    - **Change the order:** drag the pages, or go to **Settings › Profile and preferences › Your menu**.

    Home and Inbox are always in your menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite others">
    - **In a business** you invite team members by email. They log in or sign up with that address and see the invitation at the top.
    - **In a shared workspace** you invite someone from outside to work together. See [Shared](/features/shared).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free and Plus

Personal is free. **Personal Plus** adds more, such as Docs and connecting your Google Calendar. Each page says what is free and what Plus adds. Prices are on [leapla.co/pricing](https://leapla.co/pricing).

<Tip>
Light or dark? Choose it in your account menu, at the bottom of the black strip.
</Tip>
