Home is the first page you see. It sums up your day, so you know right away where you stand.

## What you see

- **A greeting** that follows the time of day, with one sentence about your day. In the evening Home already looks at tomorrow.
- **Today:** your tasks and appointments for today, including tasks you got in your businesses (with the label of that business).
- **Catch up:** tasks that are past their date. They sit together in one collapsed block, so they don't flood your day. Open it to move them to today, tomorrow or next week.
- **Shared workspaces** where something is expected from you.
- **Your businesses**, with per business how much is assigned to you, what is waiting on you and where you were mentioned.

If you mainly use Leapla for work, your businesses are at the top.

## Adjusting Home

Click **Adjust Home** to move or hide blocks. Your choice is saved on your account, so it applies on all your devices.

A block about a page that isn't in your menu doesn't appear on Home.

## Free and Plus

Home is free and is always in your menu.
