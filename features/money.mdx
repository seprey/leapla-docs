Money gives you a calm overview of what comes in and goes out, without having to connect your bank.

## What's in it

- **Overview:** how your month is going, starting from your payday.
- **Budget:** an amount per category, and how much of it is left.
- **Expenses:** what you spent or received. On a phone you add something in two taps. Search and filter by period and category, and export to a file that Excel opens.
- **Fixed costs:** what comes back every month, such as rent or a subscription. Leapla suggests fixed costs from your expenses.
- **Shared costs:** a group with housemates, friends or travel companions. Leapla works out who owes whom how much, in the shortest way.

## Free and Plus

Budget, expenses you enter yourself and sharing costs are free. **Personal Plus** adds loading a bank file, savings goals, charts and exporting.
