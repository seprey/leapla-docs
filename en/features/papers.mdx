Papers keeps track of what you would otherwise forget: when a contract ends, until when you can cancel, and what it costs.

## What's in it

- **Your papers by kind**, with a card per paper: what it is, the end date, the notice period and the cost.
- **Coming up:** the deadlines that are getting close. Leapla works out the last day to cancel by itself (end date minus notice period).
- **Reminders** in your [Inbox](/en/features/inbox), 30 days ahead by default. The deadlines are also in your [Calendar](/en/features/calendar) and on [Home](/en/features/home).
- A paper with a cost automatically becomes a fixed cost in [Money](/en/features/money).

## Free and Plus

For free you keep up to 5 papers. **Personal Plus** gives you unlimited papers, with the documents attached.
