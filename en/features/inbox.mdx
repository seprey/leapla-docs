The Inbox collects what needs your attention: reminders, deadlines, invitations, mentions and updates from your team. There is **one Inbox for all worlds**.

## Where to find it

At the bottom of the black strip on the left. The number next to it is the number of new notifications. On a phone the Inbox is in the slide-out menu.

## Filtering by world

At the top of the Inbox you choose what you see: **All**, **Personal**, or one of your businesses. If you open the Inbox from a business, the filter is already set to that business and you stay in that business.

## What you do with a notification

- **Open:** you go to the task, appointment or page the notification is about.
- **Snooze:** the notification comes back later.
- **Clear:** the notification disappears from your Inbox. What is behind it stays.

A new notification has a white border, so you see what you haven't looked at yet.

## Free and Plus

The Inbox is free and is always in your menu.
