Leapla brings your tasks, calendar, documents and notifications together, with the assistant **Apla** that works with your own data. What you keep private stays private. What you share, you share on purpose.

<Card title="Getting started" icon="rocket" href="/en/getting-started" horizontal>
  New to Leapla? From a new account to a working start in a few minutes.
</Card>

## Three worlds

In Leapla you are always in one world at a time. The narrow black strip on the left shows them all; click a world to go there.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Personal" icon="user">
    Your own day: tasks, calendar, docs and what you share. Only you see it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your businesses" icon="building">
    Each business has its own menu, with the pages that belong to that business.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shared workspaces" icon="users">
    Working together with someone from outside, with phases, steps and a conversation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
